Three rules for cold water

1. Cold water swimming is mostly about the first mi

Cold water swimming is mostly about the first minute. Breathe out slowly as you go in, keep moving, and get out before you feel heroic, because the cold keeps working on you after you are dry.

The training plan builds for eight weeks and then backs off for one. Long runs grow by about ten percent a week, easy days stay genuinely easy, and the week before the race is mostly sleep.

3. Sourdough starters are forgiving

Sourdough starters are forgiving. Feed it flour and water at roughly the same time each day, keep it somewhere that does not swing too far in temperature, and it will tell you when it is ready by doubling.

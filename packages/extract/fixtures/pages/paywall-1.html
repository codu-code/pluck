Estimating with uncertainty

A good estimate is a range with a reason. Builders who quote a single number are guessing, and the ones who survive learn to price the waste, the access and the weather before they price the materials.

Groundworks carry most of the risk on a small job. Once the trench is open you find out what the previous owner buried, and the honest estimate leaves room for at least one surprise per metre dug.

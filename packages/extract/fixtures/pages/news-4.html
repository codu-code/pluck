The archive is not a bin

By Aoife Byrne · 24 October 2026

The archive is not a bin. Things move there when you are finished with them or when they have sat untouched for long enough, and they stay searchable for as long as you keep the account.

Expiry works because it is boring and predictable. Thirty days without a touch and the item moves to the archive, unless it sits in a list, in which case it stays exactly where you put it.

Short paragraphs are a kindness. One idea each, no warm up, and a last line that tells the reader what to do with what they just learned.

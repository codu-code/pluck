The case for a shorter reading list

By Sam Rowe · 8 min read

Most of what we keep to read later, we never read. The list grows by a dozen links a week and shrinks by two, and after a year it is less a list than a record of good intentions.

The fix is not a better way to file things. It is a habit of letting things go: keep what you plucked this week close, move the rest out of sight, and trust that anything important will come back.

A short list is one you can finish. A list you can finish is one you will open.

How to actually rest

By Tom Brennan

Rest is not the absence of work. Scrolling in bed is not rest, and neither is the evening you spend half-answering email. Real rest has a shape, and it is easier to find once you know what you are looking for.

Four kinds of quiet

There is the rest of doing nothing at all. There is the rest of doing something different with your hands. There is the rest of being with people who ask nothing of you. And there is the rest of being outside, which seems to count twice.

Most weeks, we try to get all four from one sofa. Pick one this week and give it an hour with a start and an end.

Time Out Market, the long tables

Everyone in the city seems to end up here at lunch. Twenty-odd kitchens under one iron roof by the river, and long shared tables down the middle.

Go at half eleven, before the queues. Take the tables at the back, where it is quieter and the light comes in from Cais do Sodré.

Order from two places and share. The fish counters are the reason to come; the custard tarts are better in Belém.

The tile museum, Museu do Azulejo

Five hundred years of Portuguese tiles, in a convent on the edge of the city that most visitors never find.

Closed on Mondays. Give it two hours, and do not miss the long panorama of Lisbon upstairs, drawn in tile before the earthquake of 1755.

The café in the cloister is one of the calmest places in the city to sit with a coffee.

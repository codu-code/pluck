Tram 28, end to end

The old yellow tram climbs and rattles through Graça, Alfama, Baixa and Estrela, end to end in about forty minutes.

Get on at Martim Moniz before nine, or not at all. By ten the queue is longer than the ride.

Sit on the right going up. Hold on to your bag; the tram is as famous for pickpockets as for its views.

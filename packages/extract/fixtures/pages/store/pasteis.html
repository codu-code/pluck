Pastéis de Belém, the original

The custard tarts have been made to the same recipe since 1837, in a bakery that still keeps it in a locked room.

Eat them standing up, warm, with cinnamon and icing sugar from the shakers on the counter. The queue outside moves faster than it looks.

If you want to sit, go through to the tiled rooms at the back. They go on much further than you would think.

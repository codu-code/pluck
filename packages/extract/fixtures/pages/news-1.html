Small teams ship when the list is short

By Aoife Byrne · 24 October 2026

Most teams do not have a tooling problem. They have a finishing problem. Work starts quickly and stalls somewhere between the second review and the release note, and nobody can say who is holding it.

The fix is not another dashboard. It is a short list of things that are allowed to be in progress at once, written where everyone can see it, and a habit of saying out loud when something leaves the list.

We measured the time from first commit to production across forty small services. The median was three days. The slowest tenth took more than three weeks, and almost all of that time was spent waiting rather than working.

Waiting has a shape. It clusters around handoffs: a pull request that needs a second reviewer, a migration that needs a quiet window, a design question that needs one person who is on holiday.

A kitchen plan in three drafts

20 October 2026

The kitchen plan changed three times before anyone ordered a cabinet. The first version put the hob under the window, the second moved it to the island, and the third put it back because the extractor duct could not reach.

Measure the worktop cut-out twice and the appliance once. Manufacturers round their published dimensions, and a hob that is listed at five hundred and sixty millimetres will sometimes need five hundred and sixty two.

A good estimate is a range with a reason. Builders who quote a single number are guessing, and the ones who survive learn to price the waste, the access and the weather before they price the materials.

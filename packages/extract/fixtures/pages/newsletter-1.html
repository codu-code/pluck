Writing for engineers who skim

Writing for engineers who skim means leading with the decision. Put the recommendation in the first sentence, the evidence in the second paragraph, and the history at the end where only the curious will find it.

Short paragraphs are a kindness. One idea each, no warm up, and a last line that tells the reader what to do with what they just learned.

Side projects ship when the scope is smaller than your patience. Pick one boring stack, cut every feature that is not the core loop, and give yourself a Sunday night deadline that you tell someone about.

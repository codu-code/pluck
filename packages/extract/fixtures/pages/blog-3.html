Drop D, slowly

20 October 2026

Fingerstyle in drop D rewards patience. Play the pattern slowly enough that every note rings, and only then raise the tempo, a few beats per minute at a time, until the riff sits under your hands without thought.

The training plan builds for eight weeks and then backs off for one. Long runs grow by about ten percent a week, easy days stay genuinely easy, and the week before the race is mostly sleep.

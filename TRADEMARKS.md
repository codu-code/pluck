## Fine without asking

- Saying your project is "based on Pluck", "a fork of Pluck" or "works with Pluck".
- Running Pluck for yourself, your family, your team or your company, under its own name, without changing it.
- Writing about Pluck, teaching it, or linking to it, with the name and logo as they are.

## Ask first (hello@getpluck.io)

- A hosted or commercial service, or an app in a store, that uses the name Pluck or the logo.
- A changed version you hand to other people under the name Pluck.
- A name or logo close enough to be mistaken for ours.

## Forks

Fork away: give your fork its own name and logo, and remove ours from the apps, the store listings and the site. Keep
the [`NOTICE`](NOTICE) file and the licence, as the Apache License asks.

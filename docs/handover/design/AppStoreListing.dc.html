Description1482 / 4000 characters
Pluck it from any app. Tap Share, then Pluck. Pluck keeps the page as you saw it, and the app doesn't even open.

Sort it later. Your Inbox holds everything you plucked: a recipe, a longread, a travel guide, the lamp you keep meaning to buy. Swipe right for Later, left for the Archive. Tag it, put it on a List, or tick off a Todo.

The page, not just the link. Pluck keeps the text of every page, so you can read it in a clean reader, offline, long after the site has changed. Highlight the good bits.

Lists are what you're making. A trip, a kitchen, a reading list for a friend. Put things in order, write a note on each, tick off what's done, and share a List when you want to.

Find it in a second. Search covers titles, notes, tags and the full text of every page.

Nothing is lost. Things you leave untouched for 30 days move to the Archive. Nothing is deleted. You pick how long, or switch it off.

Bring your own AI. There's no AI in here. Connect the AI tools you already use, like Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor, and ask what you plucked this week, or have it tidy your Inbox while you watch. It marks everything it touched, and it can't delete a thing.

Free and open source. Use getpluck.io, or run Pluck on your own server. Export everything in one go, any time. No ads. Analytics only if you say yes.

Coming from Pocket or Omnivore? Bring your old library across in one go.

Pluck works on iPhone, in Chrome, on the web and by email. Your library is the same everywhere.

Detailed description
Pluck is a read-it-later app for everything, not just articles: a recipe from TikTok, a longread, a travel PDF, a lamp to buy, a note to self. You pluck it from the share sheet in one tap and the app never opens. Later, you sort it by hand, or ask the AI tool you already use, because Pluck has no AI inside. It connects to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and others over MCP, an open standard, and that AI can never delete anything. Things you leave alone move to the Archive after 30 days. Nothing is deleted. Pluck is free and open source, so it can't vanish the way Pocket and Omnivore did.
What is unique
A fresh take on a familiar category. Read-it-later apps either nag you or quietly delete things. Pluck does neither: untouched items go to an Archive you can search, and the share sheet saves in place without opening the app. There's no AI in the app. People bring their own AI, and every change it makes is marked and can be undone in one tap. The whole app is open source, and anyone can run their own server.
Accessibility
Dynamic Type up to the largest sizes, checked in the Simulator: rows grow and nothing clips. VoiceOver labels on the controls and the kind glyphs. Light and dark mode throughout. Animations respect Reduce Motion.

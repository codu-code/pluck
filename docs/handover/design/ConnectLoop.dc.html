Frames.

Spec.

Stage1200 × 750, ground #F6F8F4. The chat window sits at 392, 48 and is 760 × 654.
Loop10 s, infinite. Every move eases out on cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1). Typing uses steps, one per character. No bounce, no glow, no gradient.
EntrancesRise 14 px and fade in over 0.4 to 0.5 s. Small chips rise 8 px. Pluck consent rises 24 px. Presses scale to 0.96 for 0.15 s.
TypeCaptions and the consent headline in Fredoka 700, full stops in #34A07C. Everything else Geist; the link and the tool call in Geist Mono.
PluckIts own colours only on its own screens: consent, the pill, the result cards. Buttons #1E5C45, ticks #7FD3AE, ink #151A13, meta #525A4E.
The AI appUnbranded on purpose so it stands for any tool: cool greys #F3F4F6, #E4E6EA, #22252A, #6B7079, a grey send button, grey window dots, no logo, no assistant name.
At restWith reduced motion, or before the CSS loads, it shows the answer: step three, connected, three plucks back.

In the codebase.

1. Drop in the component

Three files in apps/web/components: ConnectLoop.tsx (markup, a server component), ConnectLoop.module.css (every keyframe) and ConnectLoopFit.tsx (scales the 1200 px stage to its column). No script runs the loop.

<ConnectLoopFit><ConnectLoop /></ConnectLoopFit>
2. Settings, AI tools

Replaces the drawn three-line chat in app/(app)/settings/AiToolsSection.tsx (the styles.chat block in aiSide). That closes the [CONNECTOR GIF] item in docs/launch-checklist.md and docs/design-gaps.md.

3. Connect your AI

On app/(site)/connect/page.tsx, between the hero and “Install. Thirty seconds, honestly.” The page is dark, so give it a 16 px radius and no border; the stage keeps its own light ground.

4. A video, for the README, socials or an App Store preview

node scripts/record-loop.mjs opens the page at each frame (?t= freezes it through --at), takes 300 shots at 30 fps and writes connect-loop.mp4 (H.264, CRF 18) and a connect-loop.gif with its own palette. Fonts must load, so run it where Google Fonts is reachable.

Freeze any frame

Set --at: -6.2s and --play: paused on any parent, or pass at={{6200}} to the component. That is how the stills above are drawn.

a longread · 14 min · plucked Tuesday The surprising science of habits By Maeve Kelly

Most of what you do today, you did yesterday. Not because you chose it again, but because the choice was made long ago and quietly stopped being a choice.

The trick is not more willpower. It is fewer decisions: put the guitar on its stand, not in its case.

Small changes to the room beat big promises to yourself. The runners by the door. The fruit at eye level. The phone in another room after ten.

# Design sync, 24 October 2026

The design canvas was brought in line with the app on `main` (fa939ca). This file lists what moved and what is left for the build loop.

## Already in this change

- **Icon centring.** The longer leg pulled the p low and the sparks pulled it right. The glyph now sits at its optical centre (halfway between the p's own centre and the centre of the whole mark). At 16 px and under it stays three sparks, heavier and shorter, so it never reads as two.
- **One source for the icon.** `packages/ui/src/icon.json` holds the geometry; `Icon.tsx` and `scripts/icons.mjs` both read it. `node scripts/icons.mjs` rewrites every size: web (`icon.svg`, favicons, 180, 192, 512, maskable), extension (16, 32, 48, 128 and the hollow 16 and 32), iOS (`assets/icon.png`, `icon-dark.png`, the share extension's set) and `docs/handover/brand`. It replaces `apps/ios/scripts/app-icons.mjs`.
- **Boards match the app.** Home, Connect your AI, Open source, Privacy and Terms carry the live copy. "Made in Dublin." is gone and every footer links to Support. The AI can no longer make a list public on the connector board. The extension, share sheet, iOS first run and iOS Search departures are folded in, so `docs/design-gaps.md` only lists native iOS behaviour now.
- **New boards.** Support; the Chrome and iPhone "Soon." pages; a cookie banner (not needed today, see below); five App Store screenshots at 1320 × 2868; the connect loop.
- **The connect loop**, as code: `apps/web/components/ConnectLoop.tsx`, `ConnectLoop.module.css` and `ConnectLoopFit.tsx`. Pure CSS, a server component. `scripts/record-loop.mjs` records it to MP4 and GIF from `docs/handover/motion/connect-loop.html`.
- **`support.js` fix.** The board stand-in created elements from upper-case tag names, so every `h1`, `p`, `section` and the like rendered as unknown inline elements. Chrome checks against boards before this change compared against a broken render; worth re-running the ones that looked off.

## For the build loop

1. **Put the loop in.** In `app/(app)/settings/AiToolsSection.tsx`, replace the drawn chat (`styles.chat` in `aiSide`) with `<ConnectLoopFit><ConnectLoop /></ConnectLoopFit>`. Tick `[CONNECTOR GIF]` off in `docs/launch-checklist.md` and `docs/design-gaps.md`. On `app/(site)/connect/page.tsx`, add it between the hero and "Install. Thirty seconds, honestly." with a 16 px radius. Add both routes to `e2e/design.spec.ts` and run `pnpm design`.
2. **Regenerate icons and check them.** `node scripts/icons.mjs`, then look at the favicon in a tab, the extension toolbar (hollow and filled) and the iOS home screen in the Simulator, light and dark.
3. **"Test it" names one vendor.** Settings, AI tools: the button opens claude.ai only, which breaks "never one vendor as the only option". Make it copy the test prompt ("Copied. Paste it into your AI.") or offer the same tools as the Connect tabs.
4. **Off-palette orange.** The extension's queued badge (`#C27A3A` in `apps/extension/lib/pluck.ts`) and the iOS "2 to sync" pill use an orange that is not in the palette. Use Clay `#9A5536` for "waiting" or the brand green.
5. **Cookies.** Pluck sets two cookies and both are needed, so no banner is required and Privacy already says so. Keep `Cookies.dc.html` for the day an analytics cookie arrives. Until then, optionally add "Cookies: two, both needed, neither tracks you." to the site footer.
6. **App Store.** Export `AppStore1` to `AppStore5` from the canvas at 1320 × 2868 for the 6.9 inch slot. The connect loop can be recorded as an app preview too, but App Store previews must show the app itself, so use it on the site and socials instead.

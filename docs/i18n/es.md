# Spanish (es) glossary

One Spanish for Spain and Latin America. It should read naturally in Mexico, Argentina and Spain: address the reader as **tú** (no *vosotros*, no *usted*, no voseo), and use words that work everywhere ("equipo" or "dispositivo", never "ordenador" or "computadora"; "teléfono" or "iPhone", never "móvil" or "celular").

"Pluck" stays in Latin letters as the brand everywhere. It is never conjugated in Spanish.

## Rules that hold in every string

- **Expiry copy** always says the Item moves to the Archive and nothing is deleted: *pasa al Archivo*, *no se borra nada*.
- **No exclamation marks**, neither *!* nor *¡*. Questions always open with **¿**.
- **Your AI, never one vendor alone:** *tu IA*, *herramientas de IA*. Vendors appear only in lists of examples, as in English.
- Words the English avoids stay avoided: no *guardar* or *guardado* for plucking (it is fine for a Save button in a form, where the English says "Save" too), no *marcador* for what you pluck (only for browser bookmarks you import), no *colección*, *inteligente*, *con IA*, *mágico*.
- **Product words** are capitalized as the English capitalizes them (*la Bandeja de entrada*, *el Archivo*, *una Lista*, *una Tarea*). Spanish capitalizes only the first word of a name: *Bandeja de entrada*, *Más tarde*.
- **Buttons and menu items** use the infinitive (*Recoger esta página*, *Copiar el enlace*), as Apple and Google do. **Headlines and instructions** use the tú imperative (*Recógelo*, *Toca Compartir*). The "Pluck it" button is *Recógelo* because it is the brand's line.
- **Tenses:** prefer the present (*lo que recoges*). Where a past is needed, use the simple past (*recogiste*), which is natural in Latin America and understood in Spain. Avoid the *has recogido* style where it reads Spain-only.
- **Quotes:** curly double quotes “ ”, as in the English. They are standard in Latin America and accepted by the RAE. Keep straight quotes where the English uses them in prompts and code.
- **Numbers and dates** come from the formatter. Weekdays are lowercase in Spanish (*lunes*), so put the article before them: *el {day}*, *vence el {date}*.
- **Tone:** short, plain and warm, like the English. The cheeky lines are rewritten, not translated word for word: *Gone. Well, archived.* is *Adiós. Bueno, al Archivo.* and *No, really.* is *En serio.*
- **Pluck off / Pluck it, I'm in** (the consent buttons) are *Mejor no* / *Sí, cuenta conmigo*. The pun does not survive. The Privacy page quotes these labels, so change all three places together.
- **video**, not *vídeo*: the spelling most of Latin America uses, and accepted by the RAE.
